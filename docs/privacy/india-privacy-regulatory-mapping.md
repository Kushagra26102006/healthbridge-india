# HealthBridge India — India Privacy & Healthcare Regulatory Mapping (Day 9 Task 02)

**Document Date:** 2026-11-18  
**Legal Disclaimer:** This mapping is an engineering architecture reference prepared in an offline environment without live access to the latest Gazette of India notifications. It explicitly distinguishes **enacted law**, **notified/draft rules & phased commencement**, **official ecosystem policy**, and **recommended engineering good practice**. Qualified Indian healthcare & privacy legal counsel MUST verify exact enforceability dates, statutory retention timelines, and final legal notice text before production deployment. Technical implementation alone does **not** establish DPDP compliance or ABDM certification.

| **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023** *(Enacted Statute — phased commencement via Gazette notifications; exact commencement status of subordinate rules must be verified live by counsel)* | Act No. 22 of 2023 (MeitY) | Applies to processing of digital personal data in India (Data Fiduciary / Data Processor / Consent Manager roles). | `PERSONAL`, `SENSITIVE_HEALTH_INFORMATION`, `EXTERNAL_INTEGRATION` | Versioned plain-language notice (`/privacy-policy`, `/patient/privacy-notice`), explicit granular consent (`ConsentRequestModel`, `ConsentArtifactModel`), immediate revocation (`invalidateConsentImmediately`), rights workflows (export, correction, closure/deletion at `/patient/privacy`), grievance queue (`/admin/privacy-requests`). | Counsel-approved legal notice, verified DPO/Grievance Officer appointment, consent receipts, audit trails, breach response SOP. | Whether HealthBridge qualifies as a Significant Data Fiduciary (SDF); exact statutory timeline for erasure vs clinical retention exceptions; verifiable parental consent workflow for minors/incapacitated patients. |
| **Digital Personal Data Protection Rules** *(Subordinate Rules — phased commencement & final notified text must be verified against official MeitY Gazette before launch)* | MeitY Subordinate Rules under DPDP Act, 2023 | Governs notice format, breach intimation format/timelines, retention/erasure triggers, and verifiable consent mechanics. | `PERSONAL`, `SENSITIVE_HEALTH_INFORMATION`, `AUTHENTICATION_AND_SECURITY` | Configurable retention registry (`DATA_RETENTION_POLICIES`), privacy incident/security audit monitor (`security-audit.monitor.ts`), versioned notice acknowledgements (`PrivacyNoticeAcknowledgementModel`). | Legal sign-off on exact rule commencement dates, retention windows, and CERT-In/DPBI breach intimation templates. | Exact notified breach reporting timelines to Data Protection Board of India and affected Data Principals; mandatory local retention window for access logs. |
| **Information Technology Act, 2000 & IT (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011** *(Enacted Law / Existing Rules until superseded by DPDP commencement)* | IT Act 2000 (Sec. 43A, 72A) & SPDI Rules 2011; CERT-In Directions (2022) | Applies to sensitive personal data including medical records/history and cybersecurity incident logging/reporting. | `SENSITIVE_HEALTH_INFORMATION`, `AUTHENTICATION_AND_SECURITY` | Unified security architecture (`apps/api/src/security/`), bcrypt password hashing, HTTP-only cookies, CSRF/NoSQL/file magic-byte guards, append-only `AuditLogModel` & `DataAccessEventModel`. | Independent ISO 27001 / CERT-In empanelled security audit, SIEM log retention configuration (`180 days` or counsel-verified period). | Exact transition/repeal date of Section 43A & SPDI Rules once DPDP provisions fully commence. |
